## Synthesis

The strongest common signal is not a particular palette, typeface, or visual
trend. It is a sequence:

1. discover intent and context;
2. model content, states, and constraints;
3. make a thesis and compare structurally different options;
4. let a person select when taste or product positioning is at stake;
5. preserve the result as a project contract;
6. inspect rendered evidence and revise.

The major disagreement is how much catalog or preset material to expose. This
proposal keeps catalogs as queryable references but refuses to make them the
primary source of identity. It also keeps compliance, motion, copy, handoff,
and research as specialist routes rather than mixing their rules into a
single style-generating prompt.

## Vercel case study applied

The user-provided August 31, 2026 Vercel case study is especially relevant
because it validates the operational shape rather than a specific aesthetic:

- one accessible `design.md` can carry rich guidance across environments;
- concrete examples and shipped primitives reduce interpretation drift;
- fixed prompts, inputs, model settings, and viewports make changes attributable;
- human feedback can be promoted to guidance, primitives, deterministic checks,
  or harness fixes;
- blind comparisons, holdouts, and repeated runs are more informative than a
  single impressive generation.

Their reported 39-versus-91 known-failure comparison is useful as a process
signal, not as a general quality claim: the sample was small, the checks only
covered failures already encoded, and every tested page still had a shipping
blocker. Autonomos should preserve that epistemic discipline.

## Research limitations

The repositories are self-authored practice, not controlled studies. Stars,
recency, and polished documentation are weak evidence of design outcomes. The
pilot must therefore measure process outcomes and human correction cost before
we make any artifact mandatory in the Autonomos protocol.
