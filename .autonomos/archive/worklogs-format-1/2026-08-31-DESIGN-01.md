## Key decisions and why

- Use a reusable reasoning loop plus a project-owned `DESIGN.md`, rather than a
  larger global preset library. This preserves project identity and prevents
  anti-slop rules from becoming another template.
- Require product/content grounding before high-impact visual decisions. The
  interface must be coupled to its real objects, density, states, and user job.
- Require two or three structurally divergent concepts when the direction is
  open, with a user/product-owner checkpoint or an explicitly provisional
  fallback.
- Keep accessibility, performance, motion, copy, handoff, research, and
  catalog lookup as focused specialist routes.
- Require rendered evidence, deterministic checks, at least one revision, and
  an honest `INCONCLUSIVE` result when visual observation was unavailable.
- Keep the proposal optional and project-owned until a three-surface pilot
  demonstrates stable value; do not modify the core Autonomos protocol yet.
