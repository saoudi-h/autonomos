# [PROTO-18] Make content loss and skipped recording visible to weaker agents (0.8.2)

Outcome: `status` reports tasks and issues removed or shortened since `HEAD` (or `--since <rev>`) whose words land neither in the change nor in linked files; `--strict` fails on them. Workflows say a clean status is not completion and forbid deleting content to satisfy a finding. Three `migrate` output defects fixed.

Decisions:
- Word coverage, not text equality: archiving stamps dates and relocates links, so a moved record is judged by whether 80% of its lost significant words (4+ characters) were added elsewhere or exist in files it links to. No new state file; git supplies the "before".
- Default base is `HEAD` so an agent sees the loss before committing; reviewers use `--since main`. Advisory unless `--strict`, because translation and paraphrase also lose words.

Validation:
- Real cases → ratelock INT-06 (shortened) and once-campfire-bun PERF-16 (deleted) flagged; ratelock passes once META-01 keeps the rationale.
- All 10 migrated projects since their pre-0.8 base → 6 clean; infra OCI-60 and agency PERF-09/BIZ-01/OPS-08 flagged: French text translated to English, and BIZ-01's outcome and scope genuinely dropped in round 1.
- core 137 tests, CLI 37 tests, typecheck, lint → pass.

Open: translation produces findings by design; revisit if they prove noisy. The agency BIZ-01 outcome/scope loss is left to the owner.
