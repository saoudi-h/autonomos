## Decision

The H-06 through H-11 pilot is sufficient to ship one optional `/design`
entrypoint. The protocol owns the reusable reasoning loop and evidence gates;
each project owns its product truth, visual thesis, design system, examples,
exceptions, and accepted taste in `DESIGN.md` or a linked equivalent.

The entrypoint deliberately routes instead of exposing a collection of design
workflows. It can preserve an existing direction, ask progressive questions
when the direction is open, or inventory scattered knowledge before proposing a
reviewable source → destination map. The route can change as the project
becomes clearer.

## Consumer boundary

Managed protocol files remain immutable for a declared version. Consumer
projects receive `/design` through `autonomos update`; their `DESIGN.md`,
examples, UI primitives, and existing design evidence remain project-owned and
are never overwritten. Existing knowledge extraction remains additive and
reviewable; historical worklogs, issues, and tasks are preserved.

Herald's H-10 and H-11 HTML holdouts were archived separately as static
references. Herald production source was not changed.

## Follow-up

validate the resulting contract against real multi-surface work. Do not make a
project's Cobalt direction, Herald examples, or anti-default findings global.
