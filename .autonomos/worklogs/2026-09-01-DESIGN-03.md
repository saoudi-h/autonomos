## Key decisions and why

- One entry point can expose a rich internal system; simplicity belongs at the
  interaction boundary, not in the removal of necessary design knowledge.
- `DESIGN.md` is a living contract with known, assumed, tested, and rejected
  decisions. New evidence may reopen earlier branches.
- A recurring artifact gets a frozen scenario and baseline before guidance is
  changed. A holdout is used when practical to expose regressions.
- A correction is promoted to the narrowest effective layer: project guidance,
  primitive/component, deterministic check, or evaluation harness.
- A one-off model mistake stays provisional until it repeats. Taste disputes
  without product or evidence support remain open questions.
- The validator checks contract structure only. It does not score visual
  quality and cannot replace rendered human review.
