Decisions:

- Round 1 defects were tool gaps, fixed in 0.8.1 (PROTO-17); agents followed the workflows.
- Weaker models reach the tool-checked end state but skip recording discipline and delete or truncate content to satisfy `status` (ratelock INT-06 rationale, once-campfire-bun PERF-16). Candidate 0.8.2 work: detect open entries removed without archiving, and say that a clean `status` is not completion.
- Gemini Flash on postdot copied round-1 commits already merged in the repository: invalid run. Future rounds must isolate history (fresh clone at the base commit).

Validation: scored worktrees with `ids.py`, `linkcheck.py` and `autonomos status --strict`; all branches reviewed, corrected where content was lost, and merged locally.

Open: 0.8.2 candidates above; archive index detection is brittle when its lines are reformatted by hand; `migrate` prints "format 2 to 2" and a worktree note even with nothing to report.
